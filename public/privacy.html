Spotlight360 - Privacy Policy

Hyperlocal Citizen Journalism & Community Marketplace

Your Privacy is Sacred: Spotlight360 only uses your location to discover hyperlocal news, verified citizen reports, and nearby marketplace deals within your chosen radius. We never sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: When you register via Google Sign-In or Mobile/Email, we receive your name, mobile number, email address, and profile photo.
  • Hyperlocal Location: With your permission, we obtain approximate GPS coordinates to display news and listings relevant to your neighborhood (e.g. Chennai, Ponneri, Tiruvallur).
  • Content & Media: Photos, videos, and comments uploaded by you for news verification and marketplace listings.

2. Hyperlocal Geo-Fencing & Radius Rules

Spotlight360 is built on strict locality boundaries (5km – 10km). News videos and classifieds are served according to district and neighborhood tagging to ensure content remains relevant, verified, and authentic.

3. Secure Cloud Infrastructure & Media Storage

All video reels, images, and audio attachments are transmitted via TLS 1.3 encryption and stored securely on Cloudflare R2 bucket infrastructure.

4. Marketplace & Creator Monetization Privacy

  • When you contact a Spotlight360 seller or job recruiter via WhatsApp or Call, your number is only shared directly during that interaction.
  • Creator earnings, payout history, and UPI details are strictly confidential and encrypted in our database.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have the right to request full account deletion and removal of any published video reports or listings at any time by contacting our support helpline at 9042211470.
